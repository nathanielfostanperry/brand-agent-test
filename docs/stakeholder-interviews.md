# Key Synthesis

**Synthesis & Insights**

Combating opportunism in this space through expertise, proof, and bona-fides

- Many in-sector or out of sector are “globbing” onto the narrative of rare earth, these materials, the products they create and their importance
- See a lot of money in the space
- How to continue to demonstrate their expertise — show vs tell
- Real and trustworthy, not vaporware or opportunism

Can be a hugely difficult market to enter into

- Principal Mineral is operating in the minerals/materials supply chain space
- Many big companies (or government contractors/“primes”) are hesitant to commit or buy unless capability, capacity, and scale can be proven
  - Partnerships and commitments can have major implications across a company’s supply chain — long-term production/operations implications vs. short-term supply top-up
  - Very much not a “I’ll buy X amount of copper foil, please” kind of business

Trying not to focus too hard on the capabilities (products) themselves for the Principal Mineral site more broadly

- Runs the risk of placing a limiter on PMC; in a box
- Misrepresenting the nimbleness, flexibility to build and scale and replicate with system and team and expertise across function
- Instead: focus on the solutions provider or partner narrative, showcasing capability with proof in production but the ability and capacity to collaborate on bespoke needs and solutions

Challenge: how do we address the following gaps —

- Young company in incorporation date — proof in past expertise, proven track record, validated projects/assets as part of PMC specifically as they happen
  - Make it clear: not a startup
- Feeling too much like Private Equity — we don’t want to over-index too hard on the platform, knowledge, investment, money, deals, acceleration, etc. at risk of lacking credibility as to the real tangible nature of the space
  - As a follow-up, not feeling like vaporware \- a company that is only here to invest, move money around, grow and exit
  - Avoid coming across as too cold

Principal Mineral is committed to…

- Growth
- Building an enduring capability and capacity throughout these minerals and materials spaces
- One that can be leveraged within the supply chains of the critical and core American companies, industries, processes that need it
  - The “go-to” in solving difficult mineral/material supply chain or technology problems at scale
