## Clear Space Rules

Minimum clear space (padding) must be maintained on all sides of the logo at all times.

| Variant | Clear Space Rule |
|---------|----------------|
| Lockups / Word Marks | Height of the **"P"** in "Principal Mineral" on all sides |
| Logo Mark only | Width of the **central shield shape (x)** on all sides |
| Logo Mark optical note | Because of the triangular base, optically center using the midpoint |

**Flag**: Any placement where clear space is visually violated (crowded by other elements, text, image edges).

| Background | Approved logo variant |
|-----------|----------------------|
| White | Full-color or navy (Keystone) logos |
| Light gray (Nickel) | Full-color or navy logos |
| Light blue (Powder Blue) | Navy logos |

---

## Logo Do's

- ✅ Respect minimum clear space on all sides
- ✅ Use full-color logos on white or light gray backgrounds
- ✅ Use navy logos on white, light gray, or light blue backgrounds
- ✅ Use white/light blue logos on navy or gradient backgrounds
- ✅ Scale proportionally
- ✅ Use only approved color variations from the variant files

---

## Logo Don'ts (each is a 🔴 FLAG)

- ❌ Adjust the size relationship of any logo elements
- ❌ Skew, warp, rotate, or change positioning of logos or elements
- ❌ Alter logo colors or add effects (drop shadows, outlines, glows, filters)
- ❌ Use white or light blue logos on light backgrounds
- ❌ Use full-color or navy logos on dark backgrounds
- ❌ Combine the mark with other logos in a lockup without stakeholder approval
- ❌ Recreate the logo from scratch using text/shapes instead of official files
- ❌ Use screenshots or low-resolution rasters where SVG is available

## Portfolio Differentiation

Principal Mineral is the **parent platform**. Portfolio companies (e.g., Camden Copper) operate under it but must maintain separate visual identities.

### Rules

- The Principal Mineral shield/badge mark and Cobalt/Keystone palette establish it as the parent entity
- Portfolio brands must use their **own distinct marks and color treatments**
- Principal Mineral's mark should **not be co-branded inline** with portfolio marks without approval
- In co-branded contexts, Principal Mineral appears as the **hierarchically superior** entity (larger, or positioned first/above)

### Flag Conditions

- Portfolio brand using Principal Mineral's Cobalt/Keystone without the parent mark = ⚠️ MEDIUM FLAG (may cause brand confusion)
- Principal Mineral mark altered to accommodate a portfolio brand's palette = 🔴 HIGH FLAG
- Portfolio mark combined with Principal Mineral mark at equal visual weight = ⚠️ MEDIUM FLAG (hierarchy unclear)

## Validation Checklist

When reviewing logo usage:

- [ ] Using official variant files (not recreations or screenshots)?
- [ ] Clear space respected on all sides?
- [ ] Logo at or above minimum size for its variant?
- [ ] Correct color variant for the background?
- [ ] No skew, rotation, warping, or effects applied?
- [ ] Portfolio brand differentiation maintained (if applicable)?
- [ ] No unauthorized lockups with other logos?
