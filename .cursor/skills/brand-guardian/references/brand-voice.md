# Brand Voice Reference — Principal Mineral
_Brand Guardian reference file. Load when checking or generating copy._

## Brand Personality
**Adjectives**: Trusted, Strategic, Resilient, Capable, Experienced, Unwavering, Mission-aligned

**One-sentence voice brief**: Confident and direct without being aggressive — industrial and authentic, showing capability through clarity, not volume.

---

## Core Writing Rules

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|------|----|-------|
| Lead with capability | "Domestic production at commercial scale." | "We're revolutionizing the minerals sector." |
| Be specific | Numbers, materials, facilities, outcomes | Vague adjectives and promises |
| Use active voice | "Principal Mineral builds, scales, delivers" | "Solutions are provided by our team" |
| No startup/VC language | Direct, clear, industrial | "Disrupting," "synergies," "unlocking value" |
| No performative patriotism | American purpose is inherent, not displayed | Flags-and-eagles rhetoric, "defending freedom" |
| Technical without jargon | Precise language any industry professional can read | Excessive acronyms or insider shorthand for public copy |
| Reading level | ~10th grade for public-facing content | Academic or financial-analyst density |

---

## Forbidden Words & Phrases

Flag and rewrite any of the following:

- **Startup-speak**: disrupting, revolutionizing, game-changing, innovative (as a filler adjective), transforming, pioneering (unless literal)
- **VC/PE language**: unlocking value, value creation, synergies, ecosystem (vague usage), optimizing outcomes, driving growth, scalable solutions
- **Military cosplay**: defending American freedom/liberty, protecting the homeland, fighting for America, rallying cry language
- **Hollow claims**: world-class, best-in-class, leading provider, trusted partner (unless supported by specific evidence)
- **Passive voice constructions** that obscure the subject: "solutions are delivered," "services are provided"

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Key emphasis |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Corporate / About | Confident, measured, authoritative | Track record, team credentials |
| Products / Materials | Technical, precise, solution-oriented | Material specs, capabilities |
| Investors | Substantive, strategic, growth-focused (no hype) | Real operations, not futures |
| Government / Defense | Mission-aligned, capability-focused, security-conscious | Domestic supply chain, reliability |
| Careers / Team | Purpose-driven, collaborative | Building something that matters |
| Errors / Issues | Direct, transparent, constructive | Own it, fix it |

## Voice Examples

### ✅ Good Copy

- "Domestic production of copper foil at commercial scale."
- "We design and scale bespoke solutions across minerals and materials."
- "Proven team. Real operations. National impact."
- "Reducing foreign dependencies in America's most critical supply chains."
- "A team that has already done the work." (credentialism shown through action, not claims)

### ❌ Bad Copy (with reasons)

| Bad | Why | Fix |
|-----|-----|-----|
| "Revolutionizing the critical minerals space" | Startup-speak | "Scaling domestic critical minerals production" |
| "Synergistic supply chain optimization solutions" | Jargon soup | "Integrated domestic supply for defense and manufacturing" |
| "Proudly defending American freedom through minerals" | Performative patriotism | "Reducing foreign dependencies in America's critical supply chains" |
| "Unlocking value across the materials ecosystem" | PE-speak | "Building production capability across materials and metals" |
| "World-class team of industry veterans" | Hollow claim | "A team with 30+ years of combined metals production experience" (if specific) |

## Audience Vocabulary Notes

**Defense & Government**: Use "domestic supply chain," "strategic materials," "supply chain resilience," "national security." Avoid overly corporate or financial framings.

**Energy & Manufacturing**: Use "commercial scale," "long-term supply relationships," "materials specifications." Be concrete about what you can deliver.

**Technology / Semiconductors / Aerospace**: "Advanced materials," "high-performance alloys," "precision specifications." Tech-literate audience — lean into specifics.

**Investors**: "Real operations," "proven team," "commercial-stage," "production capability." These investors are distinguishing from vaporware — speak to that directly.

## Headline Patterns (Approved Structures)

Use short, declarative structures. Avoid questions as headlines.

**Good patterns:**
- [Noun]. [Noun]. [Noun]. — e.g., "Proven team. Real operations. National impact."
- [Verb phrase that shows action] — e.g., "Scaling domestic metals production."
- [Specific claim + context] — e.g., "Copper foil production. Made in America."
- Eyebrows in ALL CAPS — e.g., "CRITICAL MINERALS" / "SUPPLY CHAIN RESILIENCE"

**Bad patterns:**
- Questions ("Are you ready for...?")
- Hyperbole ("The future of minerals is here.")
- Passive constructions ("Materials are delivered to...")
